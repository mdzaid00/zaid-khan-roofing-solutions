Common questions

Before you book.

Do I need an inspection before a repair?

An inspection or assessment helps establish the visible issue and recommended scope. The team should confirm what is needed for your specific roof.

Is the $149 inspection price final?

$149 is a proposed starting price for this website draft. Confirm the service area, inclusions, taxes, and appointment terms before enabling checkout.

Can I finance a roof replacement?

Financing may depend on availability and eligibility. Ask the business for current options, APR, fees, payment terms, and the total amount payable.

When will I receive an estimate?

Timing depends on the location, property details, and whether an on-site assessment is required. Submit your details so the team can advise you.
